NEED TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions.

Does Success Point award degrees directly?

This website does not claim that the institute is a university or degree-awarding body. Any programme must be verified with its authorised awarding institution or provider.

How can I confirm course fees and eligibility?

Contact the office and request current written details from the authorised programme provider before paying any fee.

Can I apply online?

You can submit an enquiry through this demonstration form. A real admission application process must be confirmed by the institute.

Can I become a franchise partner?

You may contact the institute to discuss a proposal. No franchise is approved or activated through this demo website.
